# How to Log In and Log Out {#how-to-log-in-and-log-out}

<p class="doc-lead">Explains the login and logout procedures.</p>

#### How to Log In {#how-to-log-in}

Access the issued URL and log in using your ID and Password.

![](/img/clarity-sc-en/image-184.png)

***

#### How to Log Out {#how-to-log-out}

Click the **Username** in the top right corner of the screen, then click the \[Sign Out] button to log out and terminate the session.

![](/img/clarity-sc-en/image-185.png)
